Coinchecker privacy

Creator: Darkartt.

Coinchecker is read-only. It does not request wallet connections, seed phrases, token approvals, or transaction signing. Your selected network, token address, and pool are sent to your chosen backend and its providers. Page content and general browsing history are not sent.

A random installation token protects access to your reports, watches, and alerts. The token stays in local browser storage; only its hash is stored by the backend. Settings lets you delete your private installation data. Public blockchain observations can remain in the shared event index and temporary cache. Provider responses are recorded as evidence.

Supported-page detection and desktop notifications require optional permissions. Detection parses supported page URLs locally and suggests a token; scanning remains your choice. Backend operators and providers can process request IP addresses; review their policies.

A report describes recorded evidence and coverage. It does not certify token, website, or wallet safety.
